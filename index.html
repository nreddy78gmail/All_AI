Frequently Asked Questions About Net Worth

What is Net Worth?

Net worth is simply what you own (assets) minus what you owe (liabilities). It is the most comprehensive measure of personal wealth.

What qualifies as an asset?

Cash, checking/savings, investment portfolios, retirement accounts (401k, IRAs), home equity, vehicles, and real property.

How can I increase my net worth?

By either increasing asset values (saving more, investing steadily, growing equity) or aggressively paying down high-interest liabilities.
